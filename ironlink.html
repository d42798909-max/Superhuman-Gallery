About

Tony Stark is an inventor, industrialist, engineer, and philanthropist. After transforming Stark Industries from a defense manufacturer into a global leader in clean energy and sustainable technology, he shifted focus to personal innovation, global security, and humanitarian initiatives.Creator of Arc Reactor technology and the Iron Man armor series, Tony co-founded The Avengers and created the Maria Stark Foundation and September Foundation to fund young innovators, grant scientific scholarships, and solve global challenges.
